Privacy Policy

Songland Studios Backup is a personal-use application that uses rclone to back up files to the owner's Google Drive account.

The application accesses Google Drive files solely to perform backups requested by the owner.

No Google user data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising.

Authentication credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer by rclone.

For privacy enquiries, contact: songlandstudios@gmail.com
